🎓 TeachByEasy — All-in-One Educational Studio with Hindsight AI Long-Term Memory

Transform static PDFs into engaging, interactive video lectures and annotated study material — now powered by persistent long-term AI memory that learns your teaching style over time.

💡 Why TeachByEasy?

Educators, teachers, tutors, and content creators frequently face friction when producing online classes or video explanations. Creating a polished lecture video often requires juggling disconnected tools: PDF viewer, annotation software, webcam recorder, and presentation teleprompter.

TeachByEasy consolidates this entire workflow in one seamless, browser-native studio. Upload any PDF slide deck, present with real-time drawing tools, insert whiteboard slides, composite your floating webcam, record crystal-clear audio/video, and export annotated lecture handouts.

🧠 Now Supercharged with Hindsight AI Memory

Traditional AI assistants have amnesia: they forget teacher preferences, target audience constraints, past corrections, and course context between sessions. TeachByEasy integrates Vectorize Hindsight
, an open-source long-term agent memory system. The AI Teaching Co-Pilot remembers your explanation style, adapts from your feedback, and continuously improves lecture notes across all your classes.

🎯 The Problems It Solves

Tool Overload	Consolidates PDF presentation, live drawing tools, webcam overlay, video recording, and AI notes into a single interface.
